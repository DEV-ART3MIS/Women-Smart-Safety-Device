Why Mobile Apps and Traditional Gadgets Fail in Real Attacks

Women’s safety technology has suffered from well-meaning but detached software designs that ignore the physiological adrenaline shock, physical violence, and spatial realities of street assaults.

The Smartphone Friction Paradox

During an ambush, adrenaline spikes heart rates to over 150 BPM, causing fine motor skill collapse. Face ID fails in dimly lit alleyways. Touchscreens do not register wet fingers. Furthermore, attackers aggressively target smartphones first to prevent victims from calling for help. Relying on an app is a fatal design flaw.

High-risk zones—such as multi-level basement parking garages, metro underpasses, transit corridors, and elevators—frequently drop standard commercial 4G/5G mobile broadband. Consumer apps stall on endless loading spinners. AegisCore utilizes low-frequency Sub-GHz NB-IoT and Cat-M1 RF waves that penetrate reinforced concrete and reach cell towers miles away.

REGULATION & BATTERY PATHWAY

Dual-Stage Power Distribution System

AUTONOMY: 14 DAYS STANDBY / 48H CONTINUOUS GPS
1. External Input
5V Micro-USB

Standard charging port compatible with any phone charger or power outlet.

2. Charge Protection
TP4056 Module

1A constant-current/constant-voltage charger with overcharge/discharge protection.

3. Energy Storage
18650 Li-ion (3.7V)

2600-3200 mAh capacity delivering reliable high-current bursts for cellular GSM transmitters.

4. Regulated Bus
AMS1117-3.3V

Clean 3.3V LDO voltage rail powering ESP32, NEO-6M, MPU6050, OLED, and MicroSD.

Power Delivery Routing:
• 3.7V Raw Battery Rail: Directly feeds the SIM800L GSM module (which requires up to 2A peak pulses during cellular transmission) through decoupling electrolytic capacitors.
• 3.3V Regulated Rail: Supplies ESP32 DevKit V1, NEO-6M GPS, MPU6050 6-Axis motion module, 0.96" OLED display, MAX9814 AGC microphone, and MicroSD card SPI board.
• Motor Driver: 3V coin vibration motor driven directly via NPN 2N2222 with flyback diode 1N4007 protection.
